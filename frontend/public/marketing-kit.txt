BODY:
Looking for a legitimate way to earn a bit extra around your day
job? The UK has dozens of genuine online side hustles - no
get-rich-quick nonsense required. Here are eleven realistic
options, what they actually pay, and how to start.

1. Freelancing your existing skills - write, design, edit video
or code on Upwork/Fiverr. Beginners start at GBP 15-30/hr and
climb fast with reviews.
2. Online surveys & microtasks - Prolific and YouGov pay
GBP 20-100/month for your opinion. Won't replace a salary, but
it's genuinely passive.
3. Tutoring & teaching - tutor UK pupils or teach English online
for GBP 15-35/hr.
4. Reselling & flipping - buy cheap from charity shops/car boots,
resell on eBay, Vinted or Facebook Marketplace. A few hundred
pounds a month is common.
5. Print-on-demand - upload designs to Redbubble or Etsy; they
print and post. Slow to build but passive once it ranks.
6. Content creation - YouTube, TikTok, a niche blog. Months of
effort before real income, but a high ceiling.
7. Virtual assistant work - GBP 12-25/hr helping small businesses
with inboxes, scheduling and admin.
8. Transcription & captioning - paid per audio minute; flexible
around other commitments.
9. Digital products - Notion templates, CV designs, presets. Make
once, sell forever via Etsy/Gumroad.
10. Pet sitting & dog walking - find clients via apps like Rover,
often GBP 10-15 per walk.
11. Cashback & matched betting - cashback on spending you'd do
anyway; matched betting (18+) carries risk, so research first.

How to choose: pick ONE that fits your skills and time, commit
for 90 days, and treat it like a small business. Track income for
HMRC - once you pass the GBP 1,000 trading allowance in a tax
year, declare it via self-assessment.
