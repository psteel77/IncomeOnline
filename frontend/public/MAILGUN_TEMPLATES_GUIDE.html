🚀 How to Add to Mailgun (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Go to Mailgun

  1. Open: https://app.mailgun.com/
  2. Log in with your credentials

Step 2: Navigate to Templates

  1. Click "Sending" in the left sidebar
  2. Click "Templates"
  3. You should see the Templates page

Step 3: Create Template 1 (Welcome!)

  1. Click the "Create Template" button
  2. Template Name: Type new-user-verification
  3. Description: Type Welcome email for new users
  4. Click the "HTML" tab or section
  5. Open Template 1 using the button above
  6. When the template opens in your browser:
    • Press Ctrl+A (Windows) or Cmd+A (Mac) to select all
    • Press Ctrl+C or Cmd+C to copy
  7. Go back to Mailgun
  8. Click in the HTML editor box
  9. Press Ctrl+V or Cmd+V to paste
  10. Click "Save Template" or "Create"

Step 4: Create Template 2 (Welcome back!)

  1. Click "Create Template" again
  2. Template Name: Type returning-user-verification
  3. Description: Type Welcome back email for returning users
  4. Click the "HTML" tab or section
  5. Open Template 2 using the button above
  6. Select all (Ctrl+A / Cmd+A)
  7. Copy (Ctrl+C / Cmd+C)
  8. Go back to Mailgun
  9. Paste in the HTML editor (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V)
  10. Click "Save Template" or "Create"

✅ Done!

Both templates are now in your Mailgun account and ready to use!

⚠️ Important: Template Variable

Both templates include this special variable:

{{VERIFICATION_LINK}}

Do not remove or change this! Mailgun will automatically replace this with your actual verification link when sending emails.

❓ Troubleshooting

Problem: Can't find Templates menu

Solution: Make sure you're in the right section: Sending → Templates

Problem: Template looks broken after pasting

Solution: Make sure you copied the ENTIRE file including <!DOCTYPE html> at the top and </html> at the bottom

Problem: Test email not arriving

Solution: In sandbox mode, you can only send to authorized recipients. Check the list above.
