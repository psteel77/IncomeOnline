🚀 How to Add Templates to Mailgun

Step 1: Access Mailgun Dashboard

  1. Go to: https://app.mailgun.com/
  2. Log in with your Mailgun credentials

Step 2: Navigate to Templates

  1. In the left sidebar, click on "Sending"
  2. Click on "Templates"
  3. You should see the Templates management page

Step 3: Create New Template

  1. Click the "Create Template" button (usually top-right)
  2. You'll see a form with fields:
    • Template Name: Enter a name (e.g., "Income Online Verification")
    • Description: Optional description
    • Template Tag: Optional tag for organization

Step 4: Add Template HTML

  1. Open one of the template links above (click the "📄 Open Template" button)
  2. Once the template opens in your browser, press Ctrl+A (Windows) or Cmd+A (Mac) to select all
  3. Press Ctrl+C or Cmd+C to copy
  4. Go back to Mailgun's "Create Template" page
  5. Find the "Template Content" or "HTML" section
  6. Click in the HTML editor
  7. Press Ctrl+V or Cmd+V to paste

⚠️ Important: Template Variables

Your templates use the following variable that Mailgun will replace:

{{VERIFICATION_LINK}}

Make sure this variable remains in the template! This is where your magic link URL will be inserted.

Step 5: Preview and Test

  1. Mailgun usually has a "Preview" button - click it to see how your email looks
  2. You can send a test email to yourself:
    • Enter your email address
    • Click "Send Test"
    • Check your inbox to verify it looks correct

Step 6: Save Template

  1. Review everything one more time
  2. Click "Create Template" or "Save"
  3. Your template is now saved and ready to use!

❓ Troubleshooting

Problem: Templates menu not showing

Solution: Make sure you're logged into the correct Mailgun account. Templates should be under "Sending" → "Templates"

Problem: Can't save template

Solution: Check that:

  • Template has a name
  • HTML content is valid
  • You have proper permissions in your Mailgun account

Problem: Email looks broken

Solution: Make sure you copied the ENTIRE HTML file including the opening <!DOCTYPE html> and closing </html> tags

Problem: Test email not arriving

Solution: In sandbox mode, you can only send to authorized recipients. Add your email to the authorized list in Mailgun settings.
